# Instrumented ordinary shopping cases

Local case audit by Luna, gpt-6-luna (max). This review covers T11, T12, T19, T20, T21, and T24 artifacts currently available. It checks saved catalog files and previews, browser handoff records, resolve/widget artifacts, and saved accessibility captures. It does not contact Muse or use browser UI. The machine-readable row-level crosswalk is [CASE-CROSSWALK.csv](../data/CASE-CROSSWALK.csv).

## What ran and what was queried

All six cases were run in the same existing chat. T11, T12, T19, T20, and T24 each have one ordinary catalog invocation; T21 has two successful catalog invocations. Each has one browser task. T24 ended access-limited after two of three product checks when the third Lulus page reached a human-verification challenge; no attempt was made to solve or bypass it. T21 call 1 returned 2 records and call 2 returned 64; the ID sets do not overlap, so the two outputs contain 66 unique records in total. Its second query was a new wording after a small successful first result, not a retry. Each catalog command requested -n 50; saved counts were 68, 71, 55, 63, 2 and 64 for T21, and 52 for T24. This contradicts an exact-50 interpretation for these outputs; it does not establish what the flag means across other conditions. No catalog call was retried. T12 had two browser spawn attempts because the first returned a non-execution availability response; the second attempt produced one completed task. T19's one browser task finished at 17:19:34Z before the later collection-control request at about 17:21 UTC. That request caused no new calls, stopped nothing, and does not turn the original completed run into a partial one. T21's saved final AX states that widget.create first returned a deferred-schema notice and was resubmitted with identical arguments; the saved final widget output reports presented=true, but the first response itself is not preserved.

The catalog rank is the file's returned ordinal, not a numeric ranking score, model-selected position, or displayed order. The raw catalog files do not expose same-run scores, and none of these ordinary calls used --raw. The preview for T19/T20 does not contain catalog descriptions; for example, T19's raw Courrèges description mentions an embroidered logo, while the saved browser handoff separately reports that product detail. T20's raw Clover Canyon description notes light makeup stains, but the saved preview did not expose that description. Exposure logs beyond the recorded previews are unavailable.

The T19 handoff reports that a product detail lists an embroidered logo, which conflicts with the shopper's no-visible-logo preference. The saved artifacts do not establish that a logo is actually visible on the garment. The final text discloses the conflict and labels Courrèges conditional, but the item was still selected and displayed. Calvin Klein is described as having no visible logos in the handoff, but page-level text, image, and browser step logs are not available for independent inspection. T19 COS is a retrieval-without-U.S.-verification example: the item appeared at rank 32 and in the preview, but exact U.S. availability was not established, so it was not selected or shown.

T20 exposes a clearer constraint miss. The shopper and browser brief require new products only. The Current Boutique result is explicitly identified as pre-owned consignment in the handoff, and the final answer repeats that fact. Even so, its browser result ID was selected, passed through resolve, and shown as the second shopping card. Calling it a wildcard and disclosing the caveat does not make it a match for the new-only request. This is an observed selection/display failure against the stated constraint; the available artifacts do not identify which internal step should have filtered it. Its catalog record has no brand field; the browser handoff identifies Clover Canyon. The other catalog rows with no brand field include Betsey Johnson. For Ulla Popken, the catalog labels the brand Angel of Style, while the page handoff reports Ulla Popken and says the catalog prefix is absent from the product page.

T21 adapts the user's “lesser-known designer labels” request into “emerging designer ... boutique label,” then “independent designer.” The first normal query succeeds but returns only Black Label Lucy and POIGNET; the second succeeds with 64 records and includes the three JNBY items selected for browser verification. The resolve input uses the second output only. All three JNBY cards are present in saved final AX; the shopper-facing prose names the Full Print and Loose Fit dresses, but not the Graphic Print card. The final prose and separate reasons file call JNBY a “real” or “genuine lesser-known designer label,” but this case supplies no brand-recognition data, popularity measure, market comparison, dated reporting, or source supporting that classification. The search words “emerging” and “independent” are query formulations, not measurements of recognition. Do not treat the label, retailer host, price, or catalog rank as popularity evidence. The third JNBY catalog record has no brand field and shows $164.50 as its price with no sale_price; the page handoff reports $235 list and $164.50 sale.

## Source integrity, timing, and limits

The run-meta files retain zero initial counters and are stale bookkeeping, not observed final counts. Actual counts (one catalog call for T11/T12/T19/T20/T24 and two for T21) are supported by the saved argv files, catalog outputs, exit statuses, browser task IDs and completion handoffs, and resolve/widget files. Browser page facts above come from saved structured handoff transcriptions, not full browser traces. The U.S. COS errors are handoff claims; the exact page response bodies and navigation logs are unavailable. The T20 browser preview capture (AX 26 and screenshot 27) shows only a partial preview state, including a blank live panel and a Betsey thumbnail, not the complete browser page history. T21's full final AX was saved as 28-T21-progress-ax.txt (not published) despite the filename implying an intermediate state; it shows all three JNBY cards and the final prose. The associated screenshot 29 shows the export summary, not the shopping cards. That export says “no retries” while the same AX documents a widget resubmission; read “no retries” as no catalog/browser retries, since the widget retry is explicitly recorded. T24 final AX 35 and screenshot 36 show only Marnella and Bronwyn as cards; Divine Allure remains unverified and unselected.
